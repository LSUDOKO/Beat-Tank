# 12 — References & Legal

## 1. IP rules (read before making anything)

**Do NOT use anywhere (game, trailer, report, store page):**
- deadmau5's name, likeness, voice, mouse-head ("mau5head") shape or any mouse-ear helmet
- World of Tanks / WoT Blitz / Wargaming names, logos, UI, tanks, camos, sounds
- The song "Familiars" or any commercial music
- Frames, textures or footage from the reference video or the Behance project
- Real brands/logos on buildings, cars or billboards

**OK to use:**
- The general idea "a music tank turns a dull city colorful" (ideas and genres aren't owned)
- Holiday themes (trees, lights, snowmen, gifts)
- Your own footage, your own music, CC0 assets, Lux3D-generated assets from your own prompts

**Code licences:** copy only from MIT / Apache-2.0 / CC0 sources; list them in `CREDITS.md`. Repos without a clear licence → learn from them, don't paste.
