# 10 — Cinematics & Trailer

All in-engine cutscenes use camera rails + scripted events. They are skippable (hold Space/A).

## 1. Intro cutscene (≈25 s, track intro low-passed)

| # | Shot | Duration | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Close-up | 3 s | VOLT's small bedroom studio (simple in-engine set: desk, laptop, two monitors, lamp, posters with our own art). VOLT taps a pad; music starts muffled |
| 2 | Over-shoulder | 3 s | Monitor shows a grey city livestream map; "SIGNAL LOST" glitch |
| 3 | Push-in | 3 s | Speakers pulse, screen glitches, cyan light floods the room |
| 4 | Whip / glitch transition | 1 s | Screen tears into equalizer bars |
| 5 | Wide, grey street | 4 s | Snow falls on silent Slush Street; frozen citizens; dead lights |
| 6 | Sky → ground | 4 s | Glitch portal opens; the Deckhound drops, lands with a bounce, snow burst |
| 7 | Low angle | 3 s | Turret hatch opens; VOLT appears wearing the cube-visor helmet (helmet "boots up") |
| 8 | Chase cam settle | 4 s | Camera swings behind the tank into gameplay; title card "SILENT NIGHT DISTRICT" |

## 2. Optional live-action opening (for the video, not the game)

Film **yourself** (or a teammate) at a real desk with headphones, ~6 s: you press a key, the screen glows, you look at camera — cut to the in-game glitch transition. Use **LuxReal** (or your editor) to create the "pulled into the game" transition and match lighting to the in-game room. This mirrors the reference's "escape from reality" idea using your own footage and persona.

## 3. THE DROP (in-game, see `02` §7)

Camera rail: crane up from behind the tank → orbit the giant tree as it lights → wide over the whole district as the remix wave spreads → back to chase cam. 2 bars total.

## 4. Boss rise (6 s)

Ground shake → plaza pit cracks → The Silencer rises with fog → music filter sweeps down → horn turns toward the player → boss name card "THE SILENCER" → gameplay.

## 6. LuxReal trailer (20–30 s)

- Use LuxReal with renders/clips from the game (tank, plaza, giant tree) to create cinematic shots that are hard to do in real time: dramatic dolly through neon streets, slow-motion confetti, close-ups of speaker cones.
- Intercut with **real gameplay** (judges need to see the real project running).
- End card: "BEATTANK — Remix the City. Built with Aholo Lux3D." + play link.

## 7. Capture checklist

- OBS: 1920×1080, 60 fps, high bitrate; game on High preset; HUD on for gameplay, off for beauty shots (add a `?nohud=1` flag).
- Record THE DROP 5+ times; pick the best.
- Record grey → neon before/after of the same street (same camera position) for the report.
